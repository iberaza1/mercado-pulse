About me

Ismael Beraza · Yukon, Oklahoma

I grew up in the Rio Grande Valley as a migrant farmworker kid. I spent seasons picking onions in the fields of New Mexico.

From 2002 to 2006 I served in the U.S. Air Force as an Airman.

After that, I worked whatever came next: roofing, serving tables at Red Lobster, and working at a pawn shop.

On the school side, I earned a BBA in Finance from the University of Oklahoma, interned at an investment counseling firm, and later earned an MS in Business Analytics from the University of Central Oklahoma.

For about ten years I've worked in banking on the operations side, not the trading floor.

Today I live in Yukon, Oklahoma. Mercado Pulse is my hobby project: a place to follow markets, banking, and a little Oklahoma ag, and to keep learning.
