What do these mean?
  • vs. S&P 500: your return minus SPY's (an ETF that tracks the S&P 500) over the same days.
  • Volatility: how bumpy your daily ups and downs are, in yearly terms.
  • Sharpe ratio: extra return per unit of bumpiness, above a 4% "safe" rate. Above 1 is good.
  • Max drawdown: the worst drop from a high point to a later low.
  • Beta: how much you move when the market moves. 1 = same as SPY, 2 = twice as much.
